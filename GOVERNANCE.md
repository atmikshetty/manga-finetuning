# Governance

The project uses a maintainer-led, consensus-seeking model. Maintainers review
changes, publish releases, triage security reports, and enforce project policy.
Routine decisions are made in pull requests. Material changes to scope,
licensing, data policy, dependencies, or governance require a public proposal
and at least seven days for comment.

When consensus cannot be reached, active maintainers decide by simple majority.
Conflicted maintainers recuse themselves. New maintainers are nominated based
on sustained, constructive contributions and approved by existing maintainers.
Inactive maintainers may move to emeritus status after six months.
