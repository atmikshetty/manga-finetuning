# Lawful Data Requirements

Use only material you created, commissioned with sufficient rights, licensed
for the intended ML use, obtained under documented permission, or determined
lawful by qualified counsel in your jurisdiction. Public availability is not a
license. Purchasing access or owning a physical copy does not automatically
grant reproduction, adaptation, or model-training rights.

Before training, document provenance, applicable terms, permitted purposes,
attribution, commercial restrictions, privacy/consent, and redistribution
rights. Apply the most restrictive relevant obligation to derivatives unless
counsel confirms otherwise.

This project provides technical tooling, not legal advice. Stop publication if
rights are unclear. Do not use the project to impersonate living artists,
mislead audiences, evade platform rules, or publish infringing substitutes.
