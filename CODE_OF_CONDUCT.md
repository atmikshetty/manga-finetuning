# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We pledge to make participation harassment-free for everyone, regardless of
age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and
expression, experience, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal
appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual and romantic identity.

## Our Standards

Positive behavior includes empathy, constructive feedback, respect for
different experiences, accountability, and focus on community benefit.
Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, sexualized attention, trolling,
insults, threats, publishing private information, and other unprofessional
conduct.

## Enforcement

Report incidents privately to the project maintainers through the repository's
security contact mechanism. Maintainers will investigate promptly, protect
reporter privacy, and may warn, temporarily restrict, or permanently ban a
participant. Decisions may be appealed to a maintainer not involved in the
initial response.

This policy adapts Contributor Covenant 2.1:
<https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct.html>.
